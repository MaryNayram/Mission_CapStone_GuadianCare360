Basic Terminologies

Data Protection in Healthcare: This means keeping patient information safe from people who shouldn’t see or use it. It includes using passwords, secure storage, and encryption to make sure health records stay private, accurate, and available when needed.

Risk Management in Healthcare: Risk management is about spotting and reducing dangers to patient data. Hospitals and clinics do this by checking for weak spots, putting safety measures in place, and having backup plans in case of cyberattacks or system failures.

Access Control in Healthcare: Access control makes sure only the right people — like doctors or nurses — can see certain patient records. This is done using passwords, fingerprint scans, or giving access based on someone’s job role.

Vulnerability Management in Healthcare: This is about finding and fixing problems in hospital computer systems before hackers can take advantage. It includes regular system checks, updates, and scanning for weaknesses.

Incident Response in Healthcare: If something goes wrong — like a data breach or virus attack — incident response is the plan to fix it quickly. It includes finding the problem, stopping it, fixing the damage, and learning how to prevent it next time.

Compliance in Healthcare: Compliance means following the rules that protect patient data. In Ghana, this includes respecting privacy laws and hospital policies, just like how HIPAA works in the U.S. It helps build trust and keeps health workers accountable.
